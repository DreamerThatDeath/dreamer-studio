06 / COMMISSION POLICY

A clear process for everyone.

Payment & revisions

  • 50% deposit is required before development begins.
  • The remaining 50% is paid after demo approval and before final file delivery.
  • Up to 3 revisions are included within the agreed scope.
  • Bug fixes for agreed features are not counted as revisions.

Payment methods

QRIS, ShopeePay, DANA, GoPay, and PayPal. Availability and any transfer fees are confirmed before payment.

Scope & delivery

  • New features outside the agreed scope may require an additional quote.
  • Estimated completion time is discussed before the project starts.
  • Domain, hosting, and third-party costs are separate unless stated.
  • Cancellation and refund terms are discussed and agreed before work begins.
